What to include

If you're reporting a measurement issue, mention the specific page or size and what you think the correct dimension should be, ideally with a source (supplier spec sheet, ANSI/ASME reference, or your own caliper measurement). For everything else — new sizes to add, broken links, advertising or partnership inquiries — a short description is enough to get started.
