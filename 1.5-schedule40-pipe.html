Good to know

Steel pipe at this size is heavy — plan hanger/support spacing so a full run doesn't sag under its own weight, especially once it's carrying liquid.

Working with 1½" Schedule 40 Steel in the field

A full-length run of filled 1½" Schedule 40 steel pipe carries significantly more weight per foot than the same size in PVC or copper, since steel itself is dense and the liquid inside adds further weight — support spacing tables for steel pipe account for this and specify closer hanger intervals than you'd use for plastic pipe of the same nominal size. Threaded joints at this size also take real torque to assemble properly; pipe wrenches sized appropriately for 1½" (rather than undersized wrenches that round off the pipe's grip surface) make a cleaner, tighter joint.
