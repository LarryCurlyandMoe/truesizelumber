Privacy Policy

This page explains what information TrueSizeLumber (truesizelumber.com) collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We don't require an account or collect the kind of personal information that would let us identify you individually.

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Information we collect

TrueSizeLumber does not have user accounts, logins, or forms that collect personal information as part of using the site's lookup tool or calculators. The site does use automated tools that collect limited technical information from every visitor, described below.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how people navigate, and general traffic trends. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as your approximate location (city/region level), device and browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is aggregated and is not used by us to identify you personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising and cookies

TrueSizeLumber may display advertising served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks in order to keep the site free to use. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Advertising partners may collect information directly from your browser or device as governed by their own privacy policies, which we don't control. We encourage you to review Google's Advertising Partners policy for more detail on how it uses data when you use our site.

Affiliate links (Amazon Associates)

TrueSizeLumber is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com. Some links on this site — mostly in the "Good to know" tips on fastener and pipe pages — are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. We only link to product categories that are directly relevant to the specific size or spec you're reading about.

Cookies in general

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. Beyond analytics and advertising cookies described above, TrueSizeLumber doesn't set cookies of its own to track you. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of the site (like saved preferences, if added in the future) may not function properly without them.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience reference tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data collected about you (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA). Because TrueSizeLumber doesn't collect identifying information directly, most requests will need to be handled through the opt-out tools linked above for analytics and advertising providers. For any other privacy question, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when adding a new advertising partner or feature. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@truesizelumber.com or via the Contact page.
