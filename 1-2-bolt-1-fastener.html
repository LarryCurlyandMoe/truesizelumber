Good to know

Torque specs for 1/2" bolts vary a lot by grade (Grade 5 vs Grade 8) — check a torque chart and use a torque wrench for anything load-bearing instead of going by feel.

Working with 1/2" × 1" Bolt in the field

At 1/2" diameter but only 1" long, this bolt is typically used where high strength is needed but the material stack is thin — automotive brackets, machine mounts, thin structural plates. Because torque-to-clamp-force relationships depend on thread condition, lubrication, and grade, a torque chart number assumes clean, lightly-oiled threads; dry or rusty threads can throw the actual clamp force off substantially even at the 'correct' torque reading. For automotive use specifically, matching the OEM-specified grade matters more than at this size than for general construction fastening, since many automotive bolts are engineered to a specific grade for a reason.
